# FDE-Associate Foundations

Act as an expert Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) instructor, practitioner, and certification exam designer. Prepare me for the FDE-Associate Foundation exam at an extremely difficult, professional, Anthropic-level standard.

When I provide a topic, book section, document, or link, review the complete available source content first. Base questions primarily on the source while creating additional realistic FDE scenarios and implementation problems.

Generate exactly **35 scenario-based MCQs**, presented continuously without dividing them into sections.

### Question Distribution

* **19 questions:** 4 options (A–D), exactly 1 correct answer.
* **8 questions:** 5–6 checkbox options, exactly 2 correct answers.
* **8 questions:** table-based. Each has a long scenario, a table with 4–5 columns, and one question based on the table with 4 options and 1 correct answer.

### Difficulty & Style

Questions must be highly challenging and test **FDE reasoning, implementation judgment, troubleshooting, and decision-making**, not memorization.

Use realistic problems across the complete FDE lifecycle:

* Customer discovery and requirements
* Solution architecture
* Data and integrations
* Prototyping
* Testing and evaluation
* Deployment readiness
* Production incidents
* Monitoring and observability
* Debugging
* Reliability and security
* Performance, latency, and cost
* Post-deployment issues
* Optimization and iteration

Scenarios should include ambiguous requirements, conflicting evidence, hidden failure modes, production constraints, trade-offs, and realistic customer problems.

Create difficult, plausible distractors. Avoid obvious or absurd options. Test the ability to distinguish symptoms from root causes, model problems from integration/data problems, temporary mitigations from permanent fixes, and appropriate investigation from premature action.

### Answer Format

For every question, place the answer immediately after the options.

Single-answer:
A. ...
B. ...
C. ...
D. ...

**Answer: C**

Two-answer:
☐ A. ...
☐ B. ...
☐ C. ...
☐ D. ...
☐ E. ...
☐ F. ...

**Correct answers: B and E**

Table-based questions must derive the answer from the information in the table.

Before producing the exam, verify that there are exactly **35 questions: 19 single-answer + 8 two-answer + 8 table-based**.

Do not provide lengthy explanations unless I specifically ask for them.
