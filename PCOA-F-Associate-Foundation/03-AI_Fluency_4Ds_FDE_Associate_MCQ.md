## Preparation Focus

Prioritize **Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence**. For FDE-style questions, focus on matching AI autonomy to the task, supplying the right context, verifying outputs and claimed actions, and applying privacy, security, transparency, and deployment safeguards.
