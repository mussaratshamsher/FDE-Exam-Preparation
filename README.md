# FDE-Associate Exam Preparation (PCAO-F)

This repository is dedicated to preparing for the **Panaversity Certified Associate: Foundations (PCAO-F)** certification. We will study the 10 core crash courses designed for this certification from the [AI Agent Factory](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f).

## 10 Crash Courses (Foundations)

1. **Just Delegate It**
2. **What AI Actually Is**
3. **AI Fluency (The 4Ds)**
4. **AI Prompting in 2026**
5. **Markdown In, HTML Out**
6. **Code You Never Write**
7. **Skills & Connectors**
8. **How to Think in the AI Era**
9. **Workflow Design & Diagnosis**
10. **Governance & Responsible Use**

## About PCAO-F

The **PCAO-F (Panaversity Certified Associate: Foundations)** validates your understanding of the foundational blueprint for building AI-Native Companies. It covers the spec-driven, human-supervised process for manufacturing, deploying, and monetizing Digital FTEs (Full-Time Equivalents) and reliable AI workers.
